Research Manuscript Submitted — 2026

Gastrointestinal Polyp Segmentation

Conducted an extensive benchmark study evaluating transformer and CNN-based semantic segmentation architectures for endoscopic mucosal imagery. Developed an ensemble model combining U-Net++, DeepLabV3+, and MAnet that substantially reduced false positives in challenging polyp geometries, achieving clinically viable delineation.

Experimental Pipeline Protocol
01 // Datasets
Kvasir-SEG and CVC-ClinicDB clinical benchmarks.
02 // Preprocess
CLAHE contrast enhancement, spatial elastic distortions, and color jitter.
03 // Training
Mixed-precision (FP16), EMA weight averaging, cosine annealing with warm restarts.
04 // Ensemble
Multi-scale Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) with soft-voting across U-Net++, DeepLabV3+, and MAnet.
05 // Explain
Grad-CAM activation overlays to verify morphological focus with clinical ground truth.
06 // Results
Achieved ~0.874 IoU and ~0.924 Dice on Kvasir-SEG test splits.
